# Clear float glass sheets — non-China container sourcing

Need: **1/4", 3/8", 1/2" clear** large sheets, container loads. China is out. Spec does not need to clone a US mill brand — decent ASTM float, much cheaper.

## Why China (and Malaysia) are dead

April 2026 US orders:

| Origin | What’s on the books |
|---|---|
| **China** | Antidumping (China-wide often **~151%**) **+** CVD **+** Sec 301. Petitioner was **Vitro** (US mill). |
| **Malaysia** | CVD **~17–102%** (Xinyi, Jinjing, NSG). AD dropped as negligible; CVD still kills it. |

Do not RFQ China, Malaysia, or Chinese-owned plants that float the glass there (origin follows the **tin bath**, not the cutting shop).

## Thickness map (what to put on the RFQ)

US inch vs what every mill actually makes:

| You sell | Metric mill size | Approx weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1/4" | **6 mm** (true 1/4" is 6.35 mm — mills sell 6 mm) | ~15 kg/m² |
| 3/8" | **10 mm** (true 3/8" is 9.53 mm) | ~25 kg/m² |
| 1/2" | **12 mm** (true 1/2" is 12.7 mm — ask if they can run 12.7) | ~30 kg/m² |

Quality: **ASTM C1036 Type I, Class 1 (clear), Q3 or Q4 glazing**. Annealed float, not tempered unless he asks later.

**Large sheets:** ask for jumbo **3210 × 6000 mm** (or US stock **96" × 130" / 84" × 120"**). Glass is **weight-limited**. Ship **20 ft** A-frame crates, not 40HQ. A 20 ft of 12 mm is roughly **700–800 m²**; 6 mm roughly **1,400–1,600 m²**.

## Duty (non-China) — still not free

Clear nonwired float, large sheets:

- **Under 10 mm** (the 6 mm / 1/4"): HTS **7005.29.18** — MFN **14.5¢/m²** (pennies) + ~**12.5%** forced-labor overlay on most origins
- **10 mm and up** (10 mm / 12 mm): HTS **7005.29.25** — MFN **4.9%** + same overlay

Turkey / India / Egypt / Indonesia are **not** on the float-glass AD/CVD orders. Overlay still applies. That is why these origins beat China: you skip the **151% dump duty**, not all duty.

**Skip Mexico as a “cheap” play.** Vitro (Mexico + US) **filed** the AD case. Guardian / Vitro / Cardinal price Mexican glass to protect US mills. Short truck is nice; the mill will not give importer pricing.

- Size: jumbo **3210 × 6000 mm** and/or cut sizes **96" × 130"** and **84" × 120"**

1. Email **Şişecam `archglass@sisecam.com`** and **Sphinx `sales@sphinxglass.com`** the same day. CC Gold Plus if you want a third number.
