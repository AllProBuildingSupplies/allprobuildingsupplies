Even if you take their **China FOB low** and land it yourself (FOB × 1.428 duty + ~$0.03 freight):

- 3/4" elbow $0.51 FOB → landed **~$0.76** vs sell $0.66 → still **underwater**
- 1/2" tee $0.38 FOB → landed **~$0.57** vs sell $0.45 → still **underwater**

Negotiation does not close this gap. To hit 50% on the 1/2" elbow they would have to sell at **~$0.17 landed** — that is Zhenpeng factory money, not a US-warehouse trader.

**USA stock vs Tommur:** on the SKUs we can price, King Smart high end is **the same neighborhood as Tommur DDP** (3/4" elbow both $0.83). You do not unlock 50–75%. You swap one expensive middleman for another, with faster ship and smaller MOQs.

## When King Smart *does* make sense

- **Samples only.** Buy a case of F2159 PPSU elbows/tees/plugs from WA stock, crimp them on Everflow/Tommur pipe, check OD, crimp go/no-go, and demand the NSF file. Fast, no container.
- **Fill-in / Amazon-style SKUs** (sillcocks, outlet boxes, tools) if you ever sell those — not this NJPD PEX box.
- **Do not** use them as the production factory for pipe + poly-alloy fittings.

Production path that actually hits ~50% on fittings: **Ningbo Zhenpeng** F2159 CTS (Lettie Chen, `chen@ppsu-pex-fittings.com`, published 1/2" elbow **$0.10 FOB**, MOQ 3000). Pipe: keep **Tommur DDP** 20 ft sticks (~44–47%) unless Palconn quotes certified 20 ft red/blue cheaper than $0.10/ft DDP.
