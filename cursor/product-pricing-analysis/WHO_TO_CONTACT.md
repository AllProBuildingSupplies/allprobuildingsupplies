This is the factory whose catalog is the Everflow clone: `1/2"PEX × 1/2"PEX Crimp Elbow, Poly Alloy, F2159`. Published **1/2" elbow $0.10 FOB**, MOQ 3000 (part B121290). ## Keep (pipe)

## Copper rings (separate)

Rings are **ASTM F1807 copper**, not PPSU. Ask Zhenpeng if they supply rings; if not: **Shangrao Olde** listed **$0.08–$0.10** (MOQ 3k–30k) or **JKL Ningbo** (`jkl@jtgroup.com.cn`, +86 199 7532 9386).

## Do not use for production

- **King Smart** — trader + WA warehouse. Fast samples only. USA-stock fittings go underwater at NJPD prices.
- **IFAN / Zhuji Fengfan** — cheap Alibaba pipe, mostly metric floor-heat, not CTS 20 ft NSF sticks.
- Anyone selling **F1960 / Uponor-style** PPSU as if it were Everflow.

## Spec line to paste in every RFQ

PEX-B, silane, **20 ft STRAIGHT** red + blue, 1/2" / 3/4" / 1" CTS SDR-9 (OD 0.625" / 0.875" / 1.125"), ASTM F876/F877, CSA B137.5, NSF-pw. Fittings: **PPSU poly-alloy, ASTM F2159 crimp + copper F1807 ring**. Not F1960. Not brass F1807 bodies. Not metric press.
