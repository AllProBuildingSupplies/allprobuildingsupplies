## Copper (Hailiang)

| Source | Public $ |
|---|---|
| Hailiang Group site | Posts copper **spot ~$14,170 / ton** (Aug 2026) — not product FOB |
| Zhejiang Hailiang / traders on Alibaba | Type K/L tube often **~$5–13 / kg** (MOQ hundreds–thousands of kg) |
| Hailiang USA / Ferguson / B.E. Atlas | **Login / call** — no open retail FOB |
| Your sheet copper FOBs | e.g. Type L ½″ **$6.93 / ft**, 1″ **$15.86 / ft** |

Rough check: ½″ Type L is on the order of ~0.13 kg/ft. At ~$8/kg Alibaba copper that is ~**$1 / ft metal**, not $7/ft — so either your copper FOBs are not pure China FOB, are wrong unit, or include big markup/freight. **Do not use the copper FOBs in the margin sheet as the factory bar without confirming with Tommur/Hailiang.**
