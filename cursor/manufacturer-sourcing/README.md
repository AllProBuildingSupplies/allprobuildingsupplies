# Manufacturer sourcing research (US certifications)

**Verdict: Tommur is not the best choice if you need verified US listings (NSF / UPC).**  
Tommur is a workable low-MOQ China factory contact for plastics, and you already have Tommur/Lesso SKUs mapped — but for potable-water and code acceptance in the US, **ERA** and **LESSO America** are stronger on plastics, **Hailiang USA** on copper, and **Rifeng** on PEX.

Research date: 2026-09-02. Always re-check live NSF / IAPMO listings before placing a container.

## Certifications that matter in the US

Do **not** treat Alibaba “NSF certificated” text as proof. Marketing claims are common; **official listings** are what inspectors, distributors, and insurers care about.

| Need | When | Where to verify |
|---|---|---|
| **NSF/ANSI 14** | Plastic pipe/fittings performance | [info.nsf.org](https://info.nsf.org/) |
| **NSF/ANSI/CAN 61** | Potable water contact (copper, CPVC, PEX, PVC pressure) | NSF PWS listings |
| **NSF/ANSI/CAN 372** | Lead-free | Often marked `[G]` on NSF 61 listings |
| **cUPC / IAPMO** | UPC jurisdictions (much of the West) | IAPMO Product Listing Directory |
| **ASTM / ASME** | Dimensional & material specs on the part | Spec + marking on product |

**DWV-only PVC** often needs NSF 14 + ASTM D2665 more than NSF 61. **Potable** copper / CPVC / PEX / PVC pressure needs **61** (and usually 14 for plastics).

## Tommur — what we found

**Company:** Tommur Industry (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. / brands TOMEX · TOMEEX  
**Alibaba:** [shtommur.en.alibaba.com](https://shtommur.en.alibaba.com/)  
**Site:** [tommur.com](https://www.tommur.com/) · [tomeex.com](https://tomeex.com/)

| Claim | Reality (checked 2026-09-02) |
|---|---|
| “UL, NSF, CE certificates” on product pages | **No NSF company/product hits** for `Tommur` or `TOMEX` in NSF Plumbing or PWS listings |
| Certificate gallery on tomeex.com | Shows ISO 9001, RoHS, CE, LFGB, FDA, SGS — **not NSF / UPC** |
| Factory size | Mid-size (~10,000 m², ~8 extruders / 24 injection machines per their About page) — real maker, not a mega US-export specialist |
| Price / MOQ | Competitive; low MOQs cited on B2B portals; good for mixed-container flexibility |
| Showroom oddity | Their own site has pages titled like “Era UPVC/PVC… NSF-Pw & Upc” — treat that as marketing noise, not proof they *are* ERA |

**Bottom line:** Fine as a **price/MOQ** supplier if you independently force certifications onto *your* SKUs (or only sell non-potable DWV where listing risk is lower). **Not** the best “buy once, sell anywhere in the US with NSF/UPC” partner.

## Best-fit manufacturers by category

### 1) PVC + CPVC (pipe & fittings) — prefer **ERA**, strong alternate **LESSO**

#### ERA Co., Ltd. / ERA Piping (Zhejiang)
- Official **NSF/ANSI 61** listing as *ERA Co., Ltd. DBA ERA PIPING (ZHEJIANG) Co., Ltd.* — PVC & CPVC fittings, Sch 40/80 pipe, valves, CTS CPVC, etc. ([NSF company 1Q980](https://info.nsf.org/Certified/PwsComponents/Listings.asp?Company=1Q980&Standard=061))
- **IAPMO/UPC** example: Sch 80 PVC fittings file **10908** (void after Dec 2029)
- IAPMO-accredited in-house lab; Americas ~40% of export revenue (company materials)
- Alibaba: ERA PIPING (ZHEJIANG) — larger scale than Tommur; higher MOQ (often carton-based)
- Best when: you need **verified NSF + UPC** on ASTM Sch 40/80 and CPVC for US potable / code work

#### LESSO America / Guangdong Liansu (China LESSO Group)
- US subsidiary in Corona, CA + coast-to-coast DCs — easier returns, stock, and submittals
- NSF listing under *Guangdong Liansu… DBA Lesso America, Inc.* and *Lesso America, Inc.*
- Publishes cNSFus / cUPC + ASTM D2466/D2467 / NSF 14 & 61 / 372 on Sch 40/80 submittals
- Your catalog already maps **Lesso codes** on PVC DWV — natural path to dual-source or switch
- Best when: you want US-facing brand + listings and can live with distributor/MOQ pricing vs pure FOB factory

**Cheapest among certified plastics:** usually **ERA direct FOB** beats LESSO America landed for container buys; LESSO can win on speed / less customs risk. Tommur may still undercut both on raw FOB — that is the certification tradeoff.

### 2) PEX-B — prefer **Rifeng** (verify exact SKU)

- **Rifeng Enterprise (Foshan)** has deep NSF 14 / 61 listings for PEX / PEXa / PEX-AL-PEX and fittings
- Match **ASTM F876/F877**, chlorine class, and fitting system (F1807 / F2159 / F1960) — PEX is a *system* listing
- Tommur/others claiming “NSF PEX” without Rifeng-level listings are high risk for potable sales

### 3) Copper tube & wrought fittings — prefer **Hailiang USA** (China mill + US arm)

- **Hailiang America Corporation** is NSF/ANSI 61 listed for copper tube (¼″–10″), ASTM B88
- US offices/stocking — better than random Mengcun Alibaba copper shops that only *claim* NSF
- Domestic brands (Mueller Streamline, Cerro, Cambridge-Lee, Elkhart) are safest for listings but usually not cheapest
- Do **not** assume Tommur copper is NSF 61 — confirm mill + listing before potable sales

### 4) Insulation
- Thermal foam (ASTM C534 / C1427). NSF not the driver. Source on price/thickness/ASTM + flame ratings separately.

## Practical buying strategy (recommended)

1. **Stop treating one factory as “the” manufacturer for everything.** Split by material:
   - Plastics potable / pressure → **ERA** (primary) or **LESSO** (US-ready)
   - PEX → **Rifeng** (or another NSF-listed mill only)
   - Copper → **Hailiang USA** (or domestic mill with NSF 61)
   - DWV-only PVC → ERA/LESSO preferred; Tommur only if you get **written NSF 14 listing numbers** for those exact SKUs
2. **Before any PO**, require:
   - NSF listing printout (company + trade name + sizes matching your order)
   - IAPMO/cUPC certificate where you sell into UPC areas
   - Marking sample photos (NSF-pw / cUPC / ASTM numbers molded on parts)
   - Material certs / lot traceability
3. **Ask Tommur in writing:** “Provide the NSF company file number and product listing URL for each SKU on our PO.” If they cannot, do not use them for potable lines.
4. **Quote comparison (same carton fill):** ERA vs LESSO factory/US vs current Tommur FOB+freight — pick landed cost *only among suppliers who pass step 2*.
5. Keep using your existing Tommur order CSVs under `cursor/product-pricing-analysis/` as the **assortment template**, but retarget the factory letters to the winners above.
